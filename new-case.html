Eligibility check

Under the Political Parties Act, a member must be a Kenyan citizen and a registered voter, and cannot belong to another party at the same time. Confirm each with the applicant.

Resignation notice

A resignation must be given in writing. The party must notify the Registrar within seven days, so a follow-up is created automatically for you.

Consent & privacy

The applicant's details are collected only to handle this service request and, for membership registration, to submit their record to the Registrar of Political Parties. They are kept confidential, used for no other purpose, and can be seen only by authorised office staff. The applicant may ask to see or correct their details.
